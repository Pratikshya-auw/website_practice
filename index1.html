Land of Himalayas

Why visit Nepal

Nepal is a breathtaking country located in South Asia, nestled between India and China. It is famous for being home to the world's highest mountains and vibrant cultural traditions. Whether you want to trek the Everest Base Camp or explore ancient temples, Nepal is the ultimate travel destination.

Top Destinations to Explore

  • Kathmandu:The historic capital city full of ancient shrines.
  • Pokhara:A stunning lakeside city with views of the Annapurna range.
  • Chitwan National Park: Perfect for jungle safaris and spotting rhinos.

How to plan your Journey?

  1. Apply for a traveler's visa before arrival.
  2. Pack sturdy hiking boots and warm thermal layers.
  3. Exchange your currency for Nepalese Rupees (NPR).
  4. Book a local guide for the best trekking experience.
